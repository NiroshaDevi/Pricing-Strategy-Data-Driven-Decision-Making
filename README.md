📦 Assignment 2 – Packaging Optimization

This task simulated a real logistics scenario:

Given product dimensions and quantities, I determined the smallest feasible shipping box from predefined options.

The solution considered:

Product footprint constraints

Stackability and nesting behavior

Practical packing layouts

Efficient use of space (not just volume math)

# 🧠 Operational Thinking Applied

Fit-first packing logic (base area before height)

Nesting of bowls and stackable items

Realistic warehouse packing assumptions

Minimizing shipping space while ensuring safety
